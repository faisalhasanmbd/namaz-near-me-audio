# Namaz Near Me: audio

Audio used by the free, ad-free **Namaz Near Me** app (free on Google Play and the App Store).

## Tafseer lectures of Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani

`tafseer_usmani.json` is the list of lectures (Surah Al-Fatiha and the short surahs of para 30). The MP3 files are in the release **`tafseer-usmani`** and are streamed by the app, one lecture at a time.

**All credit to:**
- **Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani**, who gave these Friday lectures at Jamia Masjid Bait ul Mukarram, Karachi.
- **[NooreSunnat.com](https://www.nooresunnat.com/speaker/taqi-usmani)**, who recorded them and shares them free for dawah ("Free to use, share for Sadqa-e-Jaariya"). Many more lectures are on their website; please visit them.

**Why the lectures are kept here:** the app is free and has no ads, so that nothing distracts a listener from good work. Playing the lecture inside the app, from here, keeps that. The audio is not edited and nothing is sold. If NooreSunnat.com or the speaker wishes it to be removed, please open an issue and it will be taken down.
